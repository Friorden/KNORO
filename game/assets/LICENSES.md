# Third-party assets used in KNORO

Every picture in the game comes from the packs below. Nothing is hand-drawn or
AI-generated by the project; the tools in `tools/` only copy, cut, repack and
flatten pack files (`tools/ts_prepare.py`, `tools/packs_prepare.py`).
The original pack archives live outside the game in `art_in/packs/`.
