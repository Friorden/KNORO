1. HIGH-LEVEL GAME CONCEPT

KNORO is a turn-based:

Kingdom Strategy + City Builder + Exploration RPG + Character RPG

The player does NOT control an omnipresent strategy-game cursor.

Instead, the player creates a physical ruler called the Champion.

The Champion physically travels around an isometric hex world, meets people, discovers locations, makes RPG checks, fights, carries resources home, affects diplomacy, and develops personally.

Meanwhile, the player’s kingdom has:

* a capital/city
* buildings
* population
* resources
* production
* territory
* diplomatic relationships
* eventually culture/religion/trade/etc.

The central design rule is:

Champion → World → Kingdom → Champion

Everything should connect.

A small personal decision should potentially affect the kingdom.

A kingdom-level decision should potentially affect what the Champion can do.

The prototype’s key emotional example is:

A conversation on the other side of the map can eventually feed your city.

The game should create stories from interconnected mechanics rather than disconnected minigames.

18. MOVEMENT — IMPORTANT

There were TWO competing formulas during design.

One old session proposed:

1D6 + floor(DEX / 2)

BUT the later/current implementation specification deliberately returned to:

DEX 0–2 = +0
DEX 3–4 = +1
DEX 5 = +2

Therefore for the current prototype, use:

Movement Points =
1D6 + DEX movement modifier

with:

DEX 0–2 → +0
DEX 3–4 → +1
DEX 5 → +2

Do not silently replace this with raw DEX.

Do not silently switch to floor(DEX/2) without discussing it.

This formula should eventually be playtested.
