67. UPDATED KNORO ART DIRECTION

The current visual target is:

Hand-painted ink fantasy + graphic animated-comic energy + expressive cartoon forms + readable isometric strategy game.

Earlier references included Okami-like brush/ink qualities and MOLAKI-like stylization.

The user later specifically asked for more of an Edgerunners-like graphic/cartoon energy.

Do NOT interpret this as “make KNORO cyberpunk.”

The useful qualities are:

* strong silhouettes
* expressive posing
* graphic shadow shapes
* confident outlines
* exaggerated proportions
* angular forms mixed with organic brush shapes
* energetic composition
* strong foreground/background separation
* bold value contrast
* selective saturated color
* animation-like visual clarity

KNORO itself remains:

* fantasy
* natural
* kingdom-based
* landscape-focused
* isometric/2.5D
* painterly
* ink/brush influenced
* colorful but controlled

Do not copy identifiable characters, costumes, assets, UI, compositions, logos, or other protected visual elements from any reference.

Develop an original KNORO visual language from the high-level characteristics.

Do not force every part of the game into exactly the same visual treatment.

KNORO should have three related visual layers.

STRATEGIC UI

Information-first.

Characteristics:

* clean
* tactile
* editorial
* illustrated notebook influence
* cream/off-white surfaces
* dark ink
* highly readable typography
* clear numbers
* restrained decoration

The UI exists to make complex strategy understandable.

WORLD / CITY

Much more illustrated.

Characteristics:

* cartoon silhouettes
* ink contours
* brush-like terrain
* exaggerated buildings
* irregular natural shapes
* graphic shadows
* atmospheric depth
* painterly color masses
* animated/comic energy

DIALOGUE / COMBAT

Most expressive layer.

Characters can become larger and more dramatically illustrated.

Use:

* stronger poses
* more dramatic perspective
* bigger facial/body expression
* stronger shadows
* environmental framing
* cinematic composition
* stronger contrast

This creates a natural escalation:

STRATEGY
clean and readable
↓ zoom toward character ↓
WORLD
illustrated and alive
↓ encounter ↓
DIALOGUE / COMBAT
dramatic and expressive

69. RESOLUTION / RENDERING REQUIREMENT

The user specifically complained that the existing game looked low-resolution.

The next presentation pass should investigate and fix rendering/scaling before assuming the problem is simply asset quality.

Primary design resolution:

1920 × 1080

The game should also scale sensibly on different desktop window sizes.

Check Godot:

* viewport dimensions
* stretch settings
* content scale
* texture filtering
* font rendering
* camera zoom
* Control scaling
* procedural drawing resolution
* pixel snapping if accidentally enabled
* whether a low-resolution internal viewport is being enlarged

Desired result:

* crisp UI
* crisp typography
* clean contours
* deliberate brush roughness
* no accidental blur
* no obviously enlarged low-resolution render

Important distinction:

Hand-painted ≠ blurry.

Rough brush edges should be intentional while the overall render remains sharp.

70. WORLD MAP VISUAL REDESIGN

The current world map must eventually stop looking like a debug/prototype hex board.

Hex mechanics should remain understandable, but the player should first perceive:

a landscape

and second:

a hex grid.

The hex system is the underlying gameplay structure.

Terrain should visually soften the grid.

71. HEX PRESENTATION

Do not draw every hex as an equally dominant outlined polygon.

Instead:

* terrain fills the hex
* neighboring terrain visually connects
* hex boundaries become more subtle
* selection reveals stronger boundaries
* reachable tiles can receive overlays
* hover can reveal the exact playable cell
* ownership can use border accents
* terrain silhouettes may slightly overlap internal tile boundaries

The player must still be able to determine legal movement.

Never sacrifice gameplay readability merely to hide the grid.

⸻

72. PLAINS

Plains should not be flat beige polygons.

Use combinations of:

* broad painted ground shapes
* grass tufts
* occasional flowers
* brush strokes
* subtle elevation marks
* irregular patches
* small environmental storytelling

Keep them visually quieter than forests/hills so important locations remain readable.

⸻

73. FORESTS

Forests should have strong cartoon silhouettes.

Instead of identical tree icons:

Use clusters containing:

* different tree heights
* leaning trunks
* broad crowns
* pointed crowns
* graphic shadow masses
* foreground/background overlap

A forest hex should read as a miniature illustrated grove.

Avoid excessive tiny detail.

At world zoom, silhouette matters more than individual leaves.

⸻

74. HILLS / MOUNTAINOUS TERRAIN

Use:

* angular brush ridges
* exaggerated slopes
* dark underside shadows
* overlapping shapes
* sparse line accents

The graphic-comic influence can be stronger here.

Terrain should appear sculpted rather than represented by a generic mountain icon.

⸻

75. WATER

Water should be broad and calm enough not to overwhelm the map.

Possible language:

* painted blue/teal mass
* dark shoreline ink
* sparse curved current strokes
* occasional white/cream highlight
* irregular banks

Rivers should feel geographically meaningful.

Avoid covering water with repeated wave icons.

Special locations need distinct silhouettes.

Examples:

* crooked standing stone
* collapsed arch
* broken shrine
* abandoned cart
* unusual tree
* cairn
* tower silhouette

Discovery should produce:

“What is that?”

rather than merely displaying an icon with an exclamation mark.

77. CAPITALS / SETTLEMENTS

Settlements should visually grow from the terrain.

Buildings can use exaggerated cartoon construction:

* oversized roof
* compressed walls

Do not use generic rectangular boxes.

Kingdom identity should appear through accents.

* red banner

* red painted timber accent

NOT an entirely red building.

Combat is where KNORO can use the strongest graphic animated-comic energy.

Composition:

CHAMPION                    ENEMY
       illustrated environment
---------------------------------
HP / combat information / actions

But it should not look like two sprites standing on an empty background.

Use:

* dramatic foreground shapes
* perspective
* graphic impact shadows
* environmental framing
* exaggerated attack poses
* controlled screen shake
* impact frames/flashes
* readable damage numbers

Eventually attacks can briefly exaggerate pose/perspective rather than requiring expensive full animation.

The prototype can fake energy through:

* tweening
* squash/stretch
* position offsets
* silhouettes
* impact shapes
* camera movement

This can provide strong presentation without requiring hundreds of animation frames.
